What started as a bedroom recording project evolved into one of modern psychedelic music's most distinctive sounds. Tame Impala blends psychedelic rock, electronic production, disco, pop and introspective songwriting into something that feels completely its own.

04 — Evolution

The journey.

2007

The Beginning

Tame Impala begins as Kevin Parker's recording project in Perth, Australia.

2010

InnerSpeaker

The debut album introduces the world to Tame Impala's psychedelic sound.

2012

Lonerism

A deeper and more expansive record exploring isolation, identity and the feeling of being an outsider.

2015

Currents

The sound evolves dramatically, moving toward synth-heavy psychedelic pop and becoming a defining album of the decade.

2020

The Slow Rush

Time, memory and change become central themes in Tame Impala's most polished and electronic era.
