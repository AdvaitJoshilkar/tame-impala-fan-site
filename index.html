01 / THE PROJECT

One mind.
Infinite echoes.

Tame Impala is the psychedelic music project of Australian multi-instrumentalist, songwriter and producer Kevin Parker.

What began as a home-recording project in Perth grew into one of the most distinctive sounds of the 21st century — blurring psychedelic rock, synth-pop, disco and electronic production into something unmistakably its own.

03 / THE STORY

MADE IN
THE MIND.

From bedroom recordings to global stages, Tame Impala has remained deeply tied to Kevin Parker's singular creative process. Parker writes, records, performs and produces the project's music, shaping every layer into a carefully constructed sonic universe.

The result is music that can feel simultaneously intimate and enormous — songs about change, identity, relationships and the strange feeling of watching time move.
